What you pay

A fixed fee for the task, real costs passed on at cost, and extras only when a task needs them. You see the total before you pay.

Service fee

The menu price for the task, from ₩9,000.

+

Actual costs

Items, food, taxis, deposits and postage, at cost plus 4.5% handling.

+

Extras

Night work, rush jobs, longer trips or heavy items.

=

Your quote

Sent to your chat before anything starts. Nothing is charged until you accept it.

Extras, only when needed

Your quote lists every extra by name. When two timing extras apply, they multiply: same day at night is ×1.35, then ×1.3.

Timing

  • Night work, 22:00 to 02:00+30%
  • Within 48 hours+10%
  • Within 24 hours+20%
  • Same day+35%
  • Within 3 hours+60%
  • A fixed arrival time+20%

If we can’t finish

How much of the service fee comes back depends on whether we had already set off.

  • Online and remote tasks

    Bookings, info checks, remote interpretation, online orders before they’re placed.

    Full fee back

  • On-site tasks, once we’ve set off

    Errands, in-store purchases, pickups and professional interpretation. We keep 35% of the fee, at least ₩31,000.

    Up to 65% back

  • Queueing and companion tasks, once started

    Pop-up open-runs and companion interpretation. We keep 50% of the fee, at least ₩70,000.

    Up to 50% back

Money you haven’t used for items, food or taxis always comes back. Cancellation fees charged by shops, venues or couriers can’t be refunded.

Paying

You pay once you accept the quote, with the payment link or details we send with it. Prices are in Korean won, and dollar amounts are a guide. For on-site tasks you can also pay in won, in cash.
